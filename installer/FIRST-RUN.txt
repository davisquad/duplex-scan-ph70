DUPLEX SCAN

1. Install the manufacturer's PH70 driver if you have not already done so:
   https://www.visioneer.com/drivers-and-manuals/
2. Connect and turn on the scanner, then open Duplex Scan.
3. Load your cards and click Scan pages. Review both sides and click Save JPEGs.

Defaults: 600 DPI, duplex, 180-degree rotation, skew correction, and a 0.6 mm
gap outside the card. JPEGs save directly in Pictures\Scanned Photos under
your user home folder. After saving, the review clears for the next batch.

The app runtime is included. Setup downloads NAPS2 8.6.0 directly from its
official GitHub release and verifies its SHA-256 checksum. Its licenses stay
in scanner-engine\App. Source: https://github.com/cyanfish/naps2/tree/v8.6.0

This release is unsigned. Windows may warn or Smart App Control may block it.
The installer does not change security settings or install scanner drivers.

Updates and support: https://github.com/davisquad/duplex-scan-ph70
